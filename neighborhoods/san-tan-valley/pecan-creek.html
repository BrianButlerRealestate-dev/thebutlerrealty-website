Pecan Creek (North and South)

San Tan Valley neighborhood guide • Homes, schools, daily life, and the tradeoffs

Neighborhoods/San Tan Valley/Pecan Creek

The short version

Pecan Creek is where a lot of Queen Creek overflow landed, and it still works that way. It sits in the northern part of San Tan Valley, south of Ocotillo Road around Ironwood/Gantzel, split into Pecan Creek North and Pecan Creek South, with about 1,300 homes between them. The draw is straightforward: elementary schools inside the neighborhood, four main parks connected by walking paths, and a shorter drive to Queen Creek retail and the Loop 202 than most of San Tan Valley gets.

Homes and lots

Production homes from the 2000s: stucco, tile roofs, three to five bedrooms, a lot of two-story plans with the primary suite up or down depending on builder. The larger four and five-bedroom plans are a real part of this neighborhood's housing stock. Lots are typical subdivision size, not acreage. Homes that have been updated inside (kitchen, flooring, primary bath) separate quickly from original-condition homes in the same floor plan, and buyers pay for the done work here because so much of the competition needs it.

Inspection note for this era of build: original roof underlayment, AC age, and cast-iron or early plumbing details where applicable. Get the inspection. Price what it finds.

Daily life

The parks are the amenity. There is no clubhouse and no community pool at Pecan Creek, which keeps HOA costs in a different class than Johnson Ranch or Ironwood Crossing. People use the parks, the greenbelts, and the schools' fields, and they drive to Queen Creek for the rest: Queen Creek Marketplace, the library, Horseshoe Park and Equestrian Centre, Schnepf Farms and the Olive Mill on weekends.

That tradeoff is the whole decision in Pecan Creek. Lower monthly carrying cost, more driving for entertainment and shopping.

Schools

J.O. Combs Unified School District. Harmon Elementary serves the north side and Ellsworth Elementary the south, with Combs Middle School and Combs High School a short drive. School assignments change and are set by address — verify the assigned schools for a specific home before you write an offer. Charter options nearby include Legacy Traditional and American Leadership Academy campuses.

Getting around

This is one of the better commute positions in San Tan Valley. Ironwood/Gantzel north to the Loop 202 and State Route 24, or Ocotillo west into Queen Creek and Chandler. It still slows at school hours — Ellsworth and Gantzel is a known pinch — but you start closer than the Hunt Highway neighborhoods do.

Who Pecan Creek Fits

Good fit

Families who want an elementary school they can walk to and a shorter Queen Creek commute; buyers who would rather have a larger floor plan than a community pool; sellers with an updated home, because original-condition competition is common here.

Look elsewhere if

A community pool and clubhouse are must-haves (Ironwood Crossing, Johnson Ranch), or you want Florence Unified schools specifically, or you want a brand-new build (look at the active new construction around the valley and in Florence).

Common Questions About Pecan Creek

What is the difference between Pecan Creek North and South?

They are two phases of the same master community with separate elementary schools (Harmon in the north, Ellsworth in the south) and separate HOA details. The housing stock and the commute are similar. Compare the specific street and the specific association, not just the name.

Does Pecan Creek have a community pool?

No clubhouse pool. The amenity base is parks, greenbelts and fields. If a pool matters, plan on a private pool home or a different neighborhood.

Which district is Pecan Creek in?

J.O. Combs Unified, not Florence Unified. That distinction matters to a lot of families moving within San Tan Valley.
