San Tan Heights

San Tan Valley neighborhood guide • Homes, schools, daily life, and the tradeoffs

Neighborhoods/San Tan Valley/San Tan Heights

The short version

San Tan Heights is one of the largest communities in San Tan Valley, roughly 3,800 homes built into the foothills on the west side of the valley, with the San Tan Mountains in front of you and the Superstitions behind. People buy here for the views, the trails, and the space between houses compared to the tighter northern subdivisions. They accept a longer drive to do it.

This is Florence Unified territory, not J.O. Combs. Families moving from Pecan Creek or Ironwood Crossing sometimes miss that until they look up the high school. Check it at the start.

Homes and lots

A mix of builders over a long build-out, including D.R. Horton and others, so floor plans and finish levels vary more street to street than in a single-builder community. Foothill lots can sit above the street behind them, which is where the mountain views come from and also where drainage and retaining questions need a closer look on inspection. View lots hold value differently here. A home that looks across the valley at sunset is not the same product as the same floor plan two streets down with a block wall view, and they should not be priced the same.

Daily life

The community parks and greenbelts are the everyday gathering points. San Tan Mountain Regional Park is effectively your backyard trail system — more than 10,000 acres for hiking, biking and horses. Day to day, you will drive east to Hunt Highway or north to Queen Creek for most retail. San Tan Flat, the steakhouse and live music spot on Hunt Highway, is the local institution people actually name when you ask where they go.

Schools

Florence Unified School District. San Tan Heights Elementary serves the community, with middle school assigned by address and San Tan Foothills High School as the path for older students. School assignments change and are set by address — verify the assigned schools for a specific home before you write an offer. American Leadership Academy has a San Tan campus in this part of the valley for families looking at charter options.

School closure watch: San Tan Heights Elementary appears on both of the closure recommendations that Florence Unified's school closure committee sent to the district in August 2026. Nothing has been decided, and a Governing Board vote is expected in December. Read the update.

Getting around

Be honest with yourself about this one. You are on the west and south side of the valley. Queen Creek retail is reachable, the Loop 202 via Ironwood is a real drive, and there is no short freeway option yet. The tradeoff buys you views and space. Buyers who work from home or work in Florence or Coolidge are fine. Buyers commuting daily to Tempe or Scottsdale need to drive it at their commute time and decide with their eyes open.

Who San Tan Heights Fits

Good fit

Buyers who put mountain views and trail access ahead of commute minutes; Florence Unified families; buyers who want a larger community with a range of home sizes, including smaller lower-maintenance options.

Look elsewhere if

Daily freeway commute time is your top priority (Pecan Creek, Ironwood Crossing), or you want a community pool and clubhouse as the center of the neighborhood (Johnson Ranch, Ironwood Crossing).

Common Questions About San Tan Heights

Which school district is San Tan Heights in?

Florence Unified. The high school path is San Tan Foothills, not Combs. Verify by address.

Why do some San Tan Heights listings say Queen Creek?

Postal addresses and marketing areas overlap here. The parcel is in San Tan Valley. Check county records for the actual jurisdiction and tax record.

Is there a community pool?

Association amenities have been described differently across sources. Confirm the current amenity list and HOA cost with the association for the specific home before relying on it.
