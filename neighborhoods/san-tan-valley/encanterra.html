Encanterra

San Tan Valley neighborhood guide • Homes, schools, daily life, and the tradeoffs

Neighborhoods/San Tan Valley/Encanterra

The short version

Encanterra is the resort end of San Tan Valley. It is a gated Shea Homes community built around the La Casa club — close to 60,000 square feet of dining, pools, spa, fitness and event space — a second smaller club called The Algarve, and a private Tom Lehman-designed golf course. Part of the community is open to all ages and part is Trilogy, the 55-plus section. Everyone uses the same clubs.

This is not the least expensive way to live in San Tan Valley, and the community does not pretend otherwise. The question here is not price per square foot. It is whether you will actually use what the dues pay for.

The two decisions inside Encanterra

First, section. The 55-plus Trilogy section and the all-ages section share clubs and golf, but the street you live on, the association rules, and who can occupy the home differ. Shea has at times sold new homes in the 55-plus section to buyers younger than 55 under builder policy. That policy belongs to the builder and the association and it changes. Do not rely on a blog post about it, including this one. We confirm it in writing for the specific home and section you are looking at.

Second, club economics. There is a mandatory social membership structure and an optional golf membership. Get both schedules of costs before you compare an Encanterra resale to a new build, because incentives, membership transfers, and what is included are not the same on both sides of that comparison.

Homes

Shea builds to a higher finish level than most of the valley, with open plans, larger kitchens, and owners' suites designed for main-level living. Resales range from smaller lock-and-leave plans to golf course homes with casitas and pools. Because the community opened in the late 2000s, the earliest resales are at the age where roofs, AC systems, and pool equipment need a real inspection budget.

Who Encanterra Fits

Good fit

Buyers who want a private club life — golf, spa, dining, clubs and classes — without driving to Scottsdale to get it; 55-plus buyers who want a newer home and a large clubhouse; all-ages buyers who want a gated community with that same amenity base.

Look elsewhere if

Club dues on top of an HOA do not fit the monthly budget you want to carry; you want no HOA or a rural lot; or you will not use the amenities. Paying for La Casa and never walking into it is the most common way buyers overspend in San Tan Valley.

Common Questions About Encanterra

Is Encanterra only 55-plus?

No. There is an all-ages section and a 55-plus Trilogy section inside the same gate. Confirm which section any specific home is in.

Is the golf course public?

No. It is a private club course for members and their guests. Membership costs are separate from buying the home.

Should I talk to an agent before visiting the sales office?
