Sun City Anthem at Merrill Ranch (55-plus)

Florence neighborhood guide • Homes, schools, daily life, and the tradeoffs

Neighborhoods/Florence/Sun City Anthem at Merrill Ranch

The short version

Sun City Anthem is the 55-plus northern section of the Merrill Ranch master plan in Florence. It has its own large private clubhouse for Sun City residents, and it also sits inside the larger Merrill Ranch community, so residents use the master plan's park, lakes, sports courts and water amenities as well. The Poston Butte Golf Club winds through the community. For 55-plus buyers who want a big-clubhouse lifestyle at a Florence price instead of a Queen Creek or Mesa price, this is usually the first community to look at in this part of Pinal County.

Quick facts

LocationNorth Florence, Merrill Ranch, off Hunt Highway / Anthem Way, 85132
TypeAge-restricted 55-plus section within the all-ages Merrill Ranch master plan
ClubhouseAbout 48,000 square feet, private to Sun City — fitness center, indoor and outdoor pools, restaurant and cafe, ballroom, hobby and game rooms
GolfPoston Butte Golf Club, 18-hole, about 7,300 yards, Gary Panks design, Troon-managed. Island-green par-3 is the hole people remember. Golf cost is separate from the HOA — confirm current rates with the club
SportsTennis, pickleball, bocce, horseshoes, plus Merrill Ranch basketball, sand volleyball, rock climbing wall and water park amenities in the wider community
HomesSingle-level ranch-style plans in the Sun City section; one and two-story homes are in Parkside, the all-ages section
CFD and HOABoth apply. The Merrill Ranch CFD assessment is on the tax bill and association dues apply — confirm both by parcel. This is the most important cost check in this community

Homes

Sun City Anthem homes are single-level plans designed for main-level living, stucco and tile roofs, with a lot of golf course and mountain-view lots in the mix. Views toward Poston Butte and the desert are a real feature here on clear days. Build years run through the 2000s and 2010s with continued series after, so condition and updates drive the resale spread more than floor plan alone.

As with any 55-plus purchase, confirm the age-restriction compliance paperwork and the association's occupancy rules early, including guest and caregiver provisions and any limits on occupants under the age threshold. Get it from the association, in writing.

Daily life

The clubhouse calendar is the lifestyle: clubs, classes, trivia, dances, and seasonal events, plus the restaurant so dinner does not require leaving the community. Florence Hospital is at the northeast edge of Merrill Ranch, Hunt Highway takes you north to San Tan Valley and Queen Creek, and historic Florence is a short drive south for the library, the courthouse area, and town events like the Historic Home Tour and the Junior Parada.

For a lot of buyers the comparison set is Solera at Johnson Ranch (smaller, inside an all-ages golf community, San Tan Valley) and Trilogy at Encanterra (newer, private club, higher dues). Sun City Anthem tends to win on clubhouse scale and price for the size of home. It tends to lose on distance to the East Valley. Decide which of those matters more in your actual week.

Who Sun City Anthem at Merrill Ranch Fits

Good fit

55-plus buyers who want a large clubhouse and a full social calendar, golf in the community, and a single-level home; buyers relocating to be near adult children in San Tan Valley or Queen Creek who want their own 55-plus base 20 minutes away instead of in the same subdivision.

Look elsewhere if

Total carrying cost with CFD and HOA does not fit; you want a brand-new 55-plus build (Trilogy at Encanterra, with a different dues structure); or you want to be inside the East Valley for daily family and medical routines (Solera at Johnson Ranch shortens that drive).

Common Questions About Sun City Anthem at Merrill Ranch

Is Sun City Anthem the same as Anthem at Merrill Ranch?

Sun City Anthem is the 55-plus section of the Anthem at Merrill Ranch master plan. Parkside is the all-ages section. Same master plan, different sections, different occupancy rules.

Do I pay for golf with my HOA?

No. Golf at Poston Butte is separate. The HOA and CFD cover association and district costs. Confirm current club rates directly.

Can family stay with me or move in?

Age-restricted occupancy rules apply. Confirm the current rules with the association for your household before you buy, including long-term guests and occupants under 55.
