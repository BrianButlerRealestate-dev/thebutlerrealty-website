Anthem at Merrill Ranch — Parkside (All Ages)

Florence neighborhood guide • Homes, schools, daily life, and the tradeoffs

Neighborhoods/Florence/Anthem at Merrill Ranch — Parkside

The short version

Merrill Ranch is the master plan that put modern Florence on the map for most buyers. It sits on the north side of Florence along Hunt Highway, and it is split in two: Sun City Anthem, the 55-plus section to the north, and Parkside, the all-ages section to the south. Parkside is the family side. It wraps around a large community park with sports fields, a fishing pond, a dog park, a splash pad and playgrounds, with the Poston Butte Golf Club running through the community and Florence Hospital at the northeast edge.

If you want a master plan in Florence with a real park, an active community calendar, and a golf course, this is the default starting point. Then we check the CFD.

The CFD conversation

Do this before the tour, not after. Homes at Merrill Ranch carry a CFD assessment on the county tax bill in addition to regular property tax and the HOA. It pays for infrastructure that built the community. It can amount to a real monthly difference when you convert it, and it runs with the property for a defined term. Two homes at the same list price, one inside the CFD and one outside, do not cost the same to own. We pull the full tax record — county tax, CFD, and any other special assessments — for any Merrill Ranch home you are serious about, and we put it next to the HOA so you see total carrying cost on one page.

Sellers: your buyer will find the CFD. Price and present the home with the total cost understood, and be ready with what it pays for (the park, the infrastructure, the community you are selling). Surprises kill deals. The CFD does not have to.

Homes

Parkside housing ranges from smaller single-level plans that work for first-time buyers and downsizers who do not want the 55-plus section, to two-story family homes and larger estate-series lots. Resale condition spreads widely — early 2000s originals sit a few streets from homes built in the last few years. New construction has been active in and around Merrill Ranch in recent series, so a resale buyer should always ask what the builders are offering this month before deciding a resale is the better value.

Schools

Florence Unified. Anthem Elementary serves the community, middle school is assigned by address, with Florence High School and Poston Butte High School as the high school options depending on address. School assignments change and are set by address — verify the assigned schools for a specific home before you write an offer.

Daily life

The park is genuinely large and genuinely used: fields, the pond, events, movies and community nights. Hunt Highway connects you north to San Tan Valley and Queen Creek without going through historic Florence. Dining in Florence itself is limited and local in character — places residents name include Valentino's and the spots in historic downtown — and most residents do bigger shopping runs to Queen Creek or Coolidge. Florence Hospital being at the edge of the community is a practical plus for a lot of households.

Who Anthem at Merrill Ranch — Parkside Fits

Good fit

Families who want a master plan park and pool without leaving Florence; buyers who value the golf course and the community calendar; sellers who present total cost honestly and have the condition to compete with new series nearby.

Look elsewhere if

The CFD plus HOA total does not fit your budget (look at the newer non-CFD subdivisions, or at Florence Gardens for 55-plus); you want to be in the historic town itself; or you need a fast freeway commute (start in San Tan Valley).

Common Questions About Anthem at Merrill Ranch — Parkside

What is the CFD at Merrill Ranch and how much is it?

It is a community facilities district assessment on the tax bill that repays infrastructure costs. The amount is set by parcel and declines over the district's term. I will not quote a number on a website because the wrong number costs you money — we pull the tax record for the exact home.

Is Parkside the same HOA as Sun City Anthem?

They are sections of one master plan with their own age rules and amenity details. Confirm what your section's dues include and what requires the other section's facilities.

How far is Merrill Ranch from Queen Creek shopping?

A Hunt Highway drive north into San Tan Valley and Queen Creek. Time it from the specific home. It is closer than historic Florence, farther than San Tan Valley's northern neighborhoods.
