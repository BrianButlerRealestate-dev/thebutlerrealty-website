Florence, Arizona Neighborhoods

Three markets sharing one name • Merrill Ranch, historic Florence, and new construction

Neighborhoods/Florence

Start here

Florence is the Pinal County seat, southeast of San Tan Valley on State Route 79, and it is really three different real estate markets sharing a name.

The first is historic Florence: the original town along Main Street, with adobe and territorial-era buildings, the 1891 Pinal County Courthouse, McFarland State Historic Park, and homes that range from historic properties to small established subdivisions and manufactured housing.

The second is Anthem at Merrill Ranch on the north side of town along Hunt Highway: a large master plan split between an all-ages section (Parkside) and a 55-plus section (Sun City Anthem), built around the Poston Butte Golf Club, a large community park, and Florence Hospital at its northeast edge.

The third is the growth corridor between them and west toward San Tan Valley, where builders are actively selling new homes now — Magma Ranch, Magic Ranch, and a string of newer subdivisions along Arizona Farms Road and Hunt Highway. This is where a Florence buyer today is most likely to end up comparing a new build against a resale at Merrill Ranch.

Sellers in Florence need to hear the same thing clearly: new construction is your competition. Builder incentives, rate buydowns, and quick-move-in inventory set the terms a resale has to answer. We price with that sheet in front of us.

Quick facts: Florence as a whole

LocationAbout 60 miles southeast of Phoenix, on SR-79. Roughly 25 miles to Phoenix-Mesa Gateway Airport from the north side of town.
County rolePinal County seat. County government, the courthouse, and correctional facilities are major local employers. That employment base is a real part of the housing demand here and it does not move with Phoenix market cycles the way some of the valley's demand does.
Main corridorsSR-79 (Pinal Parkway / Florence Boulevard) north-south, Hunt Highway from San Tan Valley into the Merrill Ranch area, Arizona Farms Road east-west.
SchoolsFlorence Unified School District — Florence High School, Florence Middle School, and Anthem, Florence, and Magma Ranch elementary schools, plus Poston Butte and San Tan Foothills high schools serving the northern Florence / southern San Tan Valley area. Verify by address.
MedicalFlorence Hospital near Merrill Ranch, Sun Life Family Health Center, plus Banner Ironwood in San Tan Valley to the north.
Signature eventsCountry Thunder Arizona each April at Canyon Moon Ranch, the Florence Junior Parada rodeo and parade in late November, the Historic Home Tour in February, plus the town's Fright Fest, Hometown Holiday parade, and 4th of July Freedom Fest.
LandFlorence has a real land and ranchette market beyond the subdivisions, including irrigated and desert parcels. Land here needs water, access, flood plain, and zoning diligence that a subdivision lot does not.

The Florence-specific questions to ask on any purchase

  • CFD or special assessment. Anthem at Merrill Ranch carries a community facilities district assessment on the tax bill that pays for community infrastructure. It is a meaningful annual number, it is separate from the HOA, and buyers miss it. Get the full tax record, including the CFD line, before you compare a Merrill Ranch home to a home outside the CFD.
  • Builder incentives versus resale. If you are buying new, the incentive sheet changes monthly and the lot premium, release timing, and rate buydown structure matter more than the base price. If you are selling resale, that same sheet is what your buyer is comparing you against.
  • SR-79 and Hunt Highway. Traffic into Florence is a two-lane-highway reality in places. Country Thunder weekend and rodeo weekend are not normal traffic. Drive your commute, and if a big event week matters to you, know the calendar.
  • Water and utilities by address. Town of Florence water serves much of Florence. Newer and outlying areas can differ. Confirm provider, and for land, confirm legal and physical water access in writing.
  • Flood plain. The Gila River and its washes shape the land market south and west of town. Flood maps and elevation certificates matter here in a way they do not in most subdivisions.

Straight Answers About Florence

Can I buy land in Florence, Arizona?

Yes — and Florence is one of the few places near Phoenix where land still makes financial sense. Parcels range from residential lots to larger acreage, and they attract buyers looking to build, invest, or own land at a price point that's disappeared from closer-in markets. I know which parcels are worth pursuing and which ones have issues you'd only discover after the fact. Give me a call before you start looking on your own.

What should I know before buying in Florence?

A few honest things: Florence is further from the metro core than San Tan Valley, so the commute matters if you're in the office regularly. The small-town atmosphere is real — this isn't a place with walkable shopping and restaurant rows. But if you're working remotely, retiring, or just want space and value, those tradeoffs often feel worth it. I'll give you a clear picture of what to expect so there are no surprises after you've signed.

Is Florence AZ a good place to buy a home?

For the right buyer, yes. Florence is a strong fit for remote workers, people relocating from higher cost-of-living areas, buyers seeking more land than the metro can offer, and those interested in land investment. It's not the right fit for everyone — and I won't tell you it is just to make a sale. Let's have an honest conversation about your situation and whether Florence actually makes sense for you.
