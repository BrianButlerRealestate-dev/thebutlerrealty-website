Common Questions

Straight Answers to Real Questions

What areas do you specialize in?

I specialize in San Tan Valley and Florence. I know these markets cold — from the difference between communities like Pecan Creek and Johnson Ranch to which land parcels in Florence actually make sense. If you're buying or selling in these areas, I can give you straight answers based on actual local knowledge.

Do you work with first-time buyers?

Absolutely. First-time buyers are honestly one of my favorite parts of this job. I'll walk you through the entire process, explain what you're actually buying, connect you with a great lender, and make sure you understand everything before you sign. No pressure, no rushing — just honest guidance from start to close.

What makes you different from other agents?

I don't believe in high-pressure sales tactics. I'm not going to rush you into decisions or tell you what you want to hear just to close a deal. My job is to show you exactly what you're buying, fight hard for you during negotiations, and make sure you understand these markets before you commit. Not just your agent — your advocate.

How do I get started?

Just call or text me at 480-810-9255, or email brian@thebutlerrealty.com. We'll have a conversation about what you're looking for — no obligation, no sales pitch. If working together makes sense, great. If not, that's fine too.
