Florence Unified Board Votes 5-0 to Close Three San Tan Valley Area Schools

San Tan Mountain Middle, Skyline Ranch Elementary and Copper Basin Elementary are slated to close; Florence Middle School will relocate.

Florence Unified Board Votes 5-0 to Close Three San Tan Valley Area Schools

Follow-up to our earlier coverage: “Florence Unified Committee Narrows School Merger Proposal to Four San Tan Valley Campuses” and “Four San Tan Valley Schools Named in FUSD's School Closure Recommendations”. Those pieces reported recommendations; this is the board's vote.

The Vote

The Florence Unified School District Governing Board voted 5-0 on Nov. 10. It voted twice: the first motion closed San Tan Mountain Middle School and Skyline Ranch Elementary School and moved Florence Middle School; the second closed Copper Basin Elementary School. The board also tabled a four-day instructional week, 5-0, pending more information.

Why the District Says It Acted

The district cites financial problems and a steady decline in enrollment. The budget committee that reported to the board in October said the district must cut $5 million to $7 million for the next school year, and estimated its recommended mergers would save about $5 million together. Two board members said they disliked closing schools but that spending must be cut.

Estimated Savings

From the committee's slide deck, as reported: closing Copper Basin Elementary, $145,300; closing San Tan Mountain Middle School, $1,069,210; closing either Circle Cross Ranch or Skyline Ranch Elementary, $142,500. A four-day week was estimated at about $679,000 a year, though an administrator said it could be as low as $225,000 depending on which programs stay open on the extra day, and that even the higher figure would not reach the $5 million target alone.

Schools That Were Discussed but Not Closed

San Tan Heights Elementary and Circle Cross Ranch Elementary were in the October proposal but were not closed. Circle Cross Ranch was discussed as an alternative to Skyline Ranch; the board member who made the motion said closing it could send more families to other districts.

Students, Busing and Special Education

Board members discussed sending San Tan Mountain Middle students to another middle school in the district. Students who now walk to Copper Basin, San Tan Mountain Middle or Skyline Ranch would need bus service; busing the Copper Basin neighborhood alone would add about $100,000 a year. Copper Basin hosts district-wide special education classes: classrooms for medically fragile students and for students with autism or intellectual disabilities will move to Anthem Elementary, and special education preschoolers will be split between a north-end and a south-end school by home location. None of the options studied counted teacher layoffs; the assistant superintendent said recent staff reductions came through attrition.

Buildings and Relocation

Florence Middle School will move to a site next to the Florence Elementary campus. Administrators are considering renting or leasing the closed school buildings and asked whether the Copper Basin closure could be temporary so the school could reopen later.

What Was Not Reported

The coverage reviewed did not give closure or effective dates, enrollment figures for the affected schools, or detailed attendance-boundary changes.

What's Next

The district planned a follow-up update the week of Nov. 16 and events later this school year to honor students, staff and families of the closing schools. The next regular board meeting is Dec. 8 at 6 p.m. at San Tan Foothills High School, 1255 W. Silverdale Road, open to residents. The board plans to take up the four-day week again in December.

Sources: SanTanValley.com, “Florence Unified Board Votes to Close Three San Tan Valley Area Schools”; ABC15 Arizona, “Three Florence Unified schools to close, another relocating after board vote.”
