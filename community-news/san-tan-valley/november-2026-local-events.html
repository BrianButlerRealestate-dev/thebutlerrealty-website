Things to Do in San Tan Valley This November: Holiday Markets, the Feastival, and Hikes

Here is what is happening around San Tan Valley in November 2026, compiled from the SanTanValley.com events calendar. Confirm details before you go, since community schedules can change.

Markets and Fairs

The Johnson Ranch Holiday Market is Saturday, Nov. 7, from 9 a.m. to 1 p.m. on the Walker Butte K-8 football field. Admission is free, with a $1 raffle.

The same morning, the Solera Arts and Crafts Fair runs from 9 a.m. to 1 p.m. at the Solera Community Center, 31891 N. Echo Canyon Rd. It is free.

The C.A.M.P. Farmers Market is Saturday, Nov. 7, from 8 to 11 a.m. at LifePoint Church, with a $15 suggested donation. A second C.A.M.P. market runs 9 to 10 a.m. the same day at the San Tan Valley Library.

Food, Fun, and Giving Back

The San Tan Feastival is Saturday, Nov. 21, from 5:30 to 9 p.m. in the Walmart lot, with 15 to 25 food trucks and live music. Admission is free.

The 2nd Annual Jingle Jog Holiday 5K and Toy Drive is Saturday, Nov. 28, from 7 to 11 a.m. at Poston Butte High School. It includes a free pancake breakfast, family activities, and a North Pole Vendor Village.

Movie on the Lawn is Friday, Nov. 6, from 5 to 9 p.m. at LifePoint Church. It is free, with free popcorn and other snacks for purchase.

Grateful for You, a special day at Aimee's Farm Animal Sanctuary, is Saturday, Nov. 7, from 10 a.m. to noon. It is free.

Veterans Center

The San Tan Valley Veterans Center hosts a free Pancake Breakfast on Veterans Day, Wednesday, Nov. 11, from 6 to 11 a.m. Other free November events there include a Senior Event on Social Security, Scams and Financial Literacy on Friday, Nov. 6, from 8:30 a.m. to 2:30 p.m., a Family Game Day on Saturday, Nov. 7, from 1 to 5 p.m., and a Bunco afternoon on Friday, Nov. 27, from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m.

Outdoors at San Tan Mountain Regional Park

Park entry for these programs is $10 cash or an Annual Pass, and the hikes require registration.

  • Plant Hike, Saturday, Nov. 7, 8:30 to 10:30 a.m. (limit 20).
  • Discovery Station: Maricopa County Parks Trails, Saturday, Nov. 7, 11 a.m. to noon.
  • Stars and Saguaros Hike, Friday, Nov. 13, 5 to 8 p.m. (limit 25).
  • Discovery Station, Wednesday, Nov. 18, 2 to 4 p.m., with animal ambassadors.
  • Saguaros in Sunset, Wednesday, Nov. 18, 4:30 to 6:30 p.m., a guided hike.
  • Thanksgiving Pre-game Hike, Wednesday, Nov. 25, 6 to 8 p.m. (limit 20).

Library

The San Tan Valley Library offers free programs all month in the Bronze Room, including Adventures in Story Time on Tuesdays, Toddler Time and Baby Time midweek, and Mahjong on Saturdays. The Master Gardeners of STV meet Saturday, Nov. 21, from 1:30 to 3 p.m. The full schedule is on the SanTanValley.com calendar.

Civic Calendar

The San Tan Valley Town Council meets Wednesday, Nov. 4, and Wednesday, Nov. 18, at 6 p.m. at the Pinal County San Tan Valley Complex.

A public meeting on the San Tan Mountain Regional Park master plan is Tuesday, Nov. 10, from 5 to 6:30 p.m. at the park, and the community is invited to give input.

Sources: SanTanValley.com events calendar (santanvalley.com/events), accessed November 2026.
